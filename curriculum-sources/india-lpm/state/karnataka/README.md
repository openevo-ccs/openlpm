# state/karnataka

The confirmed-open case among India's states: Karnataka's Department of
State Educational Research and Training (DSERT) runs Karnataka Open
Educational Resources (KOER), licensing the state's own curriculum materials
under Creative Commons CC BY-SA 4.0. Direct contrast with
[`../maharashtra/`](../maharashtra/), which is fee-gated and restrictive.

**Read [`../../GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md) before treating anything
here as settled fact for a state beyond Karnataka** — this repo's
founding session researched only Karnataka and Maharashtra directly; most
Indian states (including Tamil Nadu, where the research session explicitly
could not find a citable licensing policy) remain unresearched.
