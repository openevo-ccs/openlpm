# state/maharashtra

The confirmed-restrictive case among India's states: the Maharashtra State
Bureau of Textbook Production and Curriculum Research (Balbharati) asserts
copyright over its textbooks and charges third-party publishers a real,
recently-increased licensing fee — a 2020 policy revision raised
registration fees roughly seventeen-fold, drawing public objection from the
Association of Publishers and Distributors. Direct contrast with
[`../karnataka/`](../karnataka/), which licenses equivalent material openly.

**Read [`../../GOVERNANCE.md`](../../GOVERNANCE.md) before treating anything
here as settled fact for a state beyond Maharashtra.**
