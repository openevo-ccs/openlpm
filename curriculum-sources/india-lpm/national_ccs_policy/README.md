# national_ccs_policy

India's national-level institutional intelligence and (eventually)
OpenEvo-authored policy principles — the equivalent of `deutsche_lp`'s
KMK-level content and `nys_lp`'s multi-state federal-level content, adapted
for India's own two-tier structure: a national curriculum framework (NCF,
under NEP 2020) prepared by NCERT, plus real, state-to-state licensing
variation (see `../state/`).

## What's here so far

[`records/institutional-actors.yaml`](records/institutional-actors.yaml) — 3
actor records: the Ministry of Education, NCERT, and CBSE.
[`records/timeline.yaml`](records/timeline.yaml) — 2 timeline events, both
about NCERT's own licensing posture (its 2019 all-rights-reserved
state-adoption agreement, and its 2013 NROER open-license experiment) — a
real, documented tension within NCERT's own practice, not a single
consistent policy.

No `policy-principle-record`s exist yet — this folder is honestly at the
very beginning of the same kind of growth `deutsche_lp`'s and `nys_lp`'s
equivalent folders describe for themselves.
