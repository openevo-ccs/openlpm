# state/florida/ccs_policy

OpenEvo-authored policy principles, grounded in the institutional facts,
mandates, and controversies documented in `../local_policy/` — our own
stance, not a claim about what FLDOE's or the State Board's own text says.

## Contents

[`records/policy-principles.yaml`](records/policy-principles.yaml) — 3
principles: that standards avoiding a contested concept's explicit name are
less durable than standards that name it plainly (even hedged), grounded in
Florida's own four-decade pattern; that a standards-writing committee needs
disciplinary expertise proportionate to what it's writing standards for,
grounded in the 2026 committee's 18-to-1 staffing ratio; and that a
well-documented recurring controversy like Florida's is itself legitimate
Nature-of-Science/civic-reasoning classroom content, not just background
politics to keep out of the room.

All three are `status: draft` and `review_status: author-draft` — first
drafts from this repo's founding session, not yet reviewed.
