# state/tennessee/local_policy

Institutional intelligence across a full century. Unlike `pennsylvania-lpm`,
every central mandate here is state-level — Tennessee's General Assembly and
State Board of Education, not any school district.

## Contents

- [`records/institutional-actors.yaml`](records/institutional-actors.yaml) —
  4 actor records: the Tennessee General Assembly, the State Board of
  Education, TDOE, and the Tennessee Science Teachers Association.
- [`records/timeline.yaml`](records/timeline.yaml) — 21 dated events, 1925
  through 2025.
- [`records/mandates.yaml`](records/mandates.yaml) — 5 mandate records: the
  1925 Butler Act (full criminal-prohibition text), its 1967 repeal, the
  1973 "equal time" law (struck down 1975, no `supersededBy` link since it
  was invalidated by a court, not replaced by new text), the 2012 Teacher
  Protection Act (still in force), and the current 2022 state science
  standards (full evolution/heredity benchmark text, fetched directly from
  a live, confirmed, CC-BY-4.0-licensed API — a third distinct licensing
  case among this ecosystem's state repos).
- [`records/coherence-findings.yaml`](records/coherence-findings.yaml) — 2
  findings tracing how the state's own legislative strategy changed across
  all three statutes: an outright prohibition, then a mandated-alternative
  approach, then secularized "critical thinking" language.
- [`records/controversies.yaml`](records/controversies.yaml) — **3
  `policy-controversy-record` entries, chained via `recurrenceOf` into the
  deepest such chain in this ecosystem so far**: the Butler Act/Scopes Trial
  (1925-27, resolved by a technicality dismissal), the 1973 equal-time law
  (resolved by a substantive federal appellate strike-down), and the 2012
  Teacher Protection Act (resolved by simple enactment, never tested in
  court) — three genuinely different resolution mechanisms for the same
  recurring underlying question.

## A note on this founding session's research

A century-spanning archive necessarily relies more on well-corroborated
historical scholarship (Wikipedia cross-checked against primary court
citations, NCSE's own archival pages, a peer-reviewed historical journal
article) than on documents "directly fetched" the way a 2026 news article
can be — `verificationStatus: primary-source-confirmed` here means a real
historical primary source (the statute's own text, a court's own opinion)
was consulted, not that the event is recent. Several specific day-level
dates (the exact 1927 ruling date, the exact 1967 signing date, the exact
2012 effective date) are flagged `unverified-secondary-source` or noted in
`contributor_notes` where sources genuinely conflict.
