# national_ccs_policy

Federal/multi-state actors across a full century of Tennessee's
evolution-curriculum history: the ACLU (which engineered the Scopes Trial
itself in 1925 and represented Gary Scott in 1967), NCSE, the National
Association of Biology Teachers (co-plaintiff in Daniel v. Waters), and
Public.Resource.Org (relevant to this repo's own public-records research,
not to evolution content directly).
